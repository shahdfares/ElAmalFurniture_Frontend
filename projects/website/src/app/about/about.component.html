Who We Are

Born from a love
of slow living.

ElAmal started in 2012 in a small workshop in Zagazig. We wanted furniture that felt honest — no sharp edges, no disposable trends, just pieces you'd hand down to someone you love.

Today we still ask the same question before adding anything to our collection: would this make a room feel like home?

Our Mission

To make warmth
accessible.

Our mission is simple: bring honest, well-crafted furniture into as many homes as possible. We believe everyone deserves a living space that feels calm, personal, and built to last — not just those with a decorator's budget.

  • Responsibly sourced natural materials
  • Transparent, fair pricing
  • Furniture made to outlive trends
Our Vision

A world of
slower homes.

We envision a future where people choose fewer, better things. Where a home is a sanctuary rather than a storage space — and where the objects inside it carry meaning, memory, and a quiet kind of beauty.

  • Spaces that breathe and restore
  • Design that spans generations
  • Less clutter, more intention
